=======================================================
👁️ SleepGuard — AI Focus, Posture & Sleep Detection
=======================================================

HOW TO RUN ON WINDOWS:
---------------------
1. Right-click the ZIP folder and select "Extract All..."
2. Open the extracted "SleepGuard" folder.
3. Double-click "run.bat".
4. It will automatically start and open http://localhost:3000 in your browser!
5. Click "Allow" when your browser asks for webcam permission.
6. Choose your Mode at the top and click "▶ Start Monitoring".

SPECIALIZED WORK MODES:
-----------------------
- 🎬 Movie Mode: For movies & VLC (loud alarm + Picture-in-Picture)
- 💻 Code & Dev Mode: Microsleep/nodding alert + binaural bell
- 🎨 Creative Mode: Focus flow index & lo-fi chimes for designers
- 📚 Study Mode: 1.8s quick alert + lecture timestamp bookmarks
- 🧘 Posture & Health: Spine slouching detector + 20-20-20 eye strain breaks

WATCHING IN VLC OR EXTERNAL APPS:
--------------------------------
- Click "🖼️ Float Over VLC" to pop out a small Picture-in-Picture webcam window.
- It stays on top of VLC / VS Code / Netflix and alerts you if your eyes stay closed!

REQUIREMENTS:
-------------
- Windows 10 or 11
- Chrome or Edge browser (Chrome recommended)
- Laptop webcam
- No installation needed!
